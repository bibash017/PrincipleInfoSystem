About FoodBridge

FoodBridge is a web platform that connects businesses that have surplus food with the organizations that need it. Restaurants, grocery stores and bakeries post food they can't sell, and food banks, shelters and community fridges claim it before it goes to waste.

The problem

Nearly half of the food produced for Canada is wasted, and the avoidable part is worth an estimated $58 billion a year (Second Harvest, 2024). At the same time, many local organizations struggle to get enough food. Today, donations are arranged through phone calls, emails and spreadsheets, which is slow, and food often expires before anyone can collect it.

How FoodBridge helps

  • Donors post surplus food in a short form: what it is, how much, when to pick it up and where.
  • Recipient organizations search nearby donations and claim what they can use.
  • Volunteers pick up the food and deliver it.
  • Everyone can see what has been donated and how much food was rescued.
